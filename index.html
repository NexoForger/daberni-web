Accessibility

Designed to work for people with major disabilities, not as an afterthought.

The app will be built so people with different needs can request, navigate, pay, and get support independently.

Blind and low-vision

Screen-reader labels, strong contrast, scalable text, voice guidance, and flows that do not depend on sight alone.

Deaf and hard-of-hearing

Visual status updates, captioned media, text-first communication, and support that does not rely on phone calls.

Motor and mobility

Large tap targets, reduced precision requirements, switch and keyboard access, and enough time to complete actions.

Cognitive and neurodivergent

Clear language, predictable navigation, fewer surprises, optional simplified flows, and consistent error recovery.

Assistive technology

Support for screen readers, keyboard navigation, voice control, reduced motion, and accessible map alternatives.
